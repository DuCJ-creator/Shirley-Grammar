Interactive Linking Verbs Guide

am /æm/
I am ready for the presentation.
is /ɪz/
He is the coordinator for this project.
are /ɑːr/
They are very enthusiastic about the new plan.
was /wʌz/
She was the captain of the team last year.
were /wɜːr/
We were tired after the long flight.
be /biː/
You must be careful on the icy roads.
being /ˈbiːɪŋ/
He dislikes being late to meetings.
been /bɪn/
She has been a reliable friend for years.
appear /əˈpɪər/
The storm appears calm for the moment.
feel /fiːl/
The blanket feels incredibly soft.
look /lʊk/
You look sharp in that suit.
seem /siːm/
This puzzle seems complicated at first.
smell /smɛl/
The freshly baked bread smells delicious.
sound /saʊnd/
That melody sounds familiar to me.
taste /teɪst/
The soup tastes a bit too salty.
become /bɪˈkʌm/
She became an engineer after years of study.
come /kʌm/
My dreams finally came true.
fall /fɔːl/
The crowd fell silent when the music started.
get /ɡɛt/
We need to hurry because it is getting dark.
go /ɡoʊ/
The milk went sour because it was left out.
grow /ɡoʊ/
The children grew restless during the long speech.
run /rʌn/
The well run dry during the summer drought.
turn /tɜːrn/
The leaves turn red and orange in the autumn.
keep /kiːp/
Please keep quiet during the performance.
prove /pruːv/
The investment proved highly profitable.
remain /rɪˈmeɪn/
Despite the chaos, she remained calm.
stay /steɪ/
The weather will stay warm all week.
